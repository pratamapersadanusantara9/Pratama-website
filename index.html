About Us

Heritage Craftsmanship,
Modern Excellence

PT Pratama Persada Nusantara is a leading Indonesian manufacturer specializing in premium rattan furniture and handicrafts. With generations of inherited expertise, we transform natural rattan into exquisite pieces that blend traditional artistry with contemporary design.

Our commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction has earned us the trust of international buyers across Europe, North America, Australia, and Asia. Every piece is handcrafted by skilled artisans using time-honored techniques passed down through generations.
